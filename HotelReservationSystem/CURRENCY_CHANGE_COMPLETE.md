## **?? LOCALIZATION:**

### **PHP Peso Sign Characteristics:**
- **Currency Code:** PHP
- **Decimal Separator:** . (dot)
- **Thousand Separator:** , (comma)
- **Decimal Places:** 2
- **Symbol Position:** Prefix (before amount)
- **Example:** ?2,500.00

### **Display Format:**
```
Small amounts:    ?50.00
Medium amounts:   ?2,500.00
Large amounts:    ?100,000.00
Very large:       ?1,500,000.00
Zero:             ?0.00
```
